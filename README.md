# law-study-guide

**© 2026 Alexander Morales. All rights reserved.** Proprietary: shared for personal, non-commercial use and reference only. See [LICENSE](LICENSE).

A Claude skill for building cumulative, week-by-week study guides for law conversion courses (GDL, PGDL, LLB). Each week gets lecture notes, case notes, a doctrine flowchart, a one-page exam sheet and a spaced review plan, with every definition labelled by source ("Class" or "General knowledge (verify)"). The skill is a study aid only and is designed to stay within university academic integrity and AI policies.

## What's inside

- `SKILL.md`: the skill itself (instructions, guide structure, AI-policy safeguards, workflow, checks).

## Using it

Add `SKILL.md` as a skill in Claude, then send your slides, readings, seminar questions or case reports and ask Claude to build or extend your study guide. Before you start, check your own institution's generative AI and academic integrity policies.

## Disclaimer

This is a study aid. It gives no legal advice, and you remain responsible for complying with your institution's rules on AI use and assessment.
